About

I build open-source hardware and firmware, mostly around theatre technology: DMX lighting networks, wireless crew comms and the electronics that hold a show together. Alongside that are home-automation and radio projects built on ESP32 boards.

Each project page says plainly what has and hasn't been tested, and credits the original authors where I've built on someone else's work. Everything is on GitHub; issues and pull requests are welcome.

Open-source hardware and firmware by DefeatedWarrior. Documentation pages are rendered from each project's repository.
