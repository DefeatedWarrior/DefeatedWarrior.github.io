Glossary

Term Meaning
Art-Net Lighting-control protocol carrying DMX over UDP (port 6454)
sACN / E1.31 Streaming ACN, DMX over IP with multicast
DMX512 Lighting control serial protocol, 250 kbaud over RS-485, 512 channels per universe
RDM Remote Device Management, bidirectional configuration over DMX
Universe One DMX512 stream of 512 channels
HTP / LTP Highest / Latest takes precedence: how multiple sources are merged
RS-485 Differential serial bus standard used by DMX
Bias Pull-up/pull-down pair that holds an idle RS-485 line in a known state
Termination 120 Ω resistor at the end of a line to stop reflections
Fail-safe Receiver output stays valid when the bus is open, shorted or idle
TVS Transient voltage suppressor, clamps ESD and surges
PoE / 802.3af Power over Ethernet, up to 15.4 W at the source
PD Powered device (the receiving end of PoE)
Magjack RJ45 connector with built-in magnetics
LCSC Parts distributor tied to JLCPCB
DRC / ERC Design / electrical rules check in KiCad
OTA Over-the-air firmware update
Ghost light A single bulb left on in a dark theatre
