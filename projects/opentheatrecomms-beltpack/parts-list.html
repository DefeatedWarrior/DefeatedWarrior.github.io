Parts list

Every one of the 71 parts in the CadQuery reference model, grouped as in the model. The interference checker runs a pairwise clash test over all of them. Bought parts are modelled as datasheet envelopes; a few sizes are typical values still to be checked against the exact part you buy (flagged in the description where the model says so).

Overall envelope with the TPU caps and belt plate: 108.2 × 80.7 × 39.3 mm.

Enclosure

Part Details Made
Front shell
front_shell		 PETG/ASA. Curved ribbed front, both round ends, bottom face. Holds the main PCB on 5 heat-set M2 inserts, the knob wells and the XLR. 3D printed
Back shell
back_shell		 Hip-side skin with the battery opening and two dovetail rails for the belt plate. 4 x M2 screws. 3D printed
Top plate
top_plate		 Carries the keycaps, OLED and top PCB as one module; 4 x M2 countersunk screws at the ends, hidden under the TPU cap. 3D printed
TPU top cap
tpu_top		 TPU 95A in the department colour; stretches over the top/bottom 7 mm and hides the end screws. 3D printed
TPU bottom cap
tpu_bottom		 TPU 95A bumper; cut round the XLR flange and every bottom opening. 3D printed
Belt plate
belt_plate		 PETG. Slides up onto the rails until the tongue clicks; doubles as the battery door. Belt slots take up to 38 mm. 3D printed
