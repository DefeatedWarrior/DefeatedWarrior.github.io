OpenTheatreComms beltpack: KiCad starter pack

Three ready-to-open KiCad projects, one per board, generated from the CadQuery reference model. They were written with KiCad 7 and open in KiCad 7, 8 and 9 (newer versions offer to upgrade the files on first save).

Folder Board Size Build
top_pcb/ Top PCB under the keys 99.8 × 26.5 mm curved pill 1.6 mm, 2 layers
main_pcb/ Main PCB behind the front 87.5 × 64.5 mm stepped 1.6 mm, 4 layers
pack_lid_pcb/ Battery pack pad board 24 × 8 mm 0.8 mm, 2 layers, ENIG

What's already in each project

  • Edge.Cuts: the exact outline with true arcs (front edge R427.4 and back edge R402.6 on the top board).
  • Mounting holes: M2 NPTH footprints (H1…) at the positions the case expects. They are fixed by the case, so leave them where they are.
  • Keep-outs: on the main board, a no-copper rule area along the left-hand strip (x < −39.5), where the ESP32-S3's Wi-Fi antenna and 915 MHz antenna B both sit.
  • Placement guides: Place.F (User.1) shows front/top-side parts, Place.B (User.2) shows back/bottom-side parts and Case.Ref (User.3) shows keycaps, dials, button caps, the OLED glass, the battery outline and case bosses. Notes and height limits are on Cmts.User.
  • Datum: the grid origin and the drill/place origin are set to the board datum, so the status bar coordinates match placement.csv (flip the sign of Y: KiCad's Y points down).
  • 3D: a locked, board-only footprint REF** carries 3d/enclosure_ref.step, so View → 3D Viewer shows the case, dials, keycaps, XLR and battery pack around the board. Keep the 3d/ folder next to the project folders.
  • Schematic: an empty sheet with the title block filled in.

Suggested workflow

  1. Draw the schematic, assign footprints, then Update PCB from Schematic.
  2. Fill in the ref column of the project's placement_map.csv with your references (U1, J3, SW2…). Add rot in degrees if you want to set rotations too.
  3. In the PCB editor open Tools → Scripting Console and run exec(open(r"<path>/place_parts.py").read()). Every listed footprint jumps to its position and side. Positions are part centres; footprints anchored on pin 1 (the PEC12R, for example) need a small nudge.
  4. Route, then check the fit in the 3D viewer against the enclosure.

Frames

  • Top PCB: viewed from above, F.Cu = top side. Origin at the board centre (15 mm behind the case front); +X right, +Y toward the hip edge. Encoders at (±37.5, +1.7) on B.Cu, shafts pointing down into the dial hubs.
  • Main PCB: viewed from the front, F.Cu = front face. Origin at the case centre; +Y up. The bottom edge is at Y = −36.0.
  • Pack pad board: viewed from the front, same frame as the main PCB.

Height limits

Board Side Limit
Top F (top) 3.0 mm; 2.5 under keycap flanges (the PTS815 itself); 1.4 under the OLED glass (it sits on 1.5 mm foam)
Top B (bottom) Encoders 6.5 mm; nothing else inside R17.4 of each dial axis; about 4 mm elsewhere
Main F (front) 5.0 mm; 4.0 mm behind the button recess (|x| < 29, 7.7 < y < 27.1)
Main B (back) 3.7 mm over the battery pack (x −13.8…39.8, y −31.3…15.3); about 15 mm elsewhere

Things the case expects

  • Bottom edge, main PCB F side: USB4105 centred x 9.0 with the mouth 0.8 past the edge; PJ-320D at x 22.0 with the nose 2.1 past; side tact switch at x −3.0 with the actuator 0.4 past; 3 mm LED at x 32.0, axis 2.0 off the board, bent 90°.
  • Front buttons: PTS815 at (−18, 17.4), (0, 17.4), (18, 17.4) on the main PCB F side.
  • Board link: 10-way JST SH. BM10B (top entry) on the top board B side at (−10, −1.35); SM10B (side entry) on the main board B side at the tab top, x 8, mouth pointing up. Use a 50 mm cable; it folds behind the tab.
  • Radio, with switched antenna diversity: LR2021 ANT_LF → unpopulated pi match → pSemi PE4259 SPDT at (25.8, 4.0) → U.FL A at (31, 10.5) and U.FL B at (24.5, 10.5), all on the main board B side. Put 100 pF DC-block footprints on the switch ports (fit or bridge per the datasheet) and drive CTRL from an ESP32 GPIO. Antenna A sits inside the right end, about 8 mm past the board edge; antenna B sits inside the left end, 3 mm past the edge, which is why that strip has no copper.
  • Pogo pins: three Mill-Max 0906 at (6/12/18, −15), B side, through-hole; they compress 0.8 mm onto the pack pads.

Check before layout

Typical sizes not yet confirmed against a drawing: PEC12R body height (6.5), the XLR D-flange (31 × 26 × 2) and depth (21), the USB4105 body, the PTS815 height variant, the side tact switch, the JST SH bodies, and where your OLED panel's active area sits on its glass.

Also in this pack

  • dxf/: the outlines (*_edge_cuts.dxf) and the full guide drawings, if you prefer to import graphics yourself.
  • drawings/: dimensioned drawings of each board and two sections through the case.
  • placement.csv: every part centre, envelope and height, plus the holes.
