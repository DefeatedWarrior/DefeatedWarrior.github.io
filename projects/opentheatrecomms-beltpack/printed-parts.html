Printed parts

19 parts are 3D printed. Together they use about 92 cm³ of material before infill and supports. TPU 95A parts are printed in the department colour; the structural shells are PETG or ASA. Every part is exported as an STL in cad/out/stl/ in the repository.

Assembly notes from the model

  • The front shell holds the main PCB on 5 heat-set M2 inserts; the back shell closes with 4 × M2 screws.
  • The top plate carries keycaps, OLED and top PCB as a single module, fixed by 4 × M2 countersunk screws at the ends that are hidden under the TPU cap.
  • The belt plate slides up onto two dovetail rails until its tongue clicks, and doubles as the battery door. Belt slots take belts up to 38 mm.
  • The battery tray is the back of the case: hooks at the top, snap at the bottom, locked in by the belt plate.
  • The power plunger sits 0.2 mm below the bottom face so it is hard to knock; a long press turns the pack on and off.
  • Dials are a hollow TPU tyre bonded to a PETG hub, with a D-bore on the encoder's 6 mm flatted shaft.
